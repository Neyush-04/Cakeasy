**Search volumes are not listed on purpose.** Don't guess them. After Search
Console has 4–8 weeks of data, check *Performance › Queries*; use Google Ads
Keyword Planner (free with an Ads account) for rough volumes. Replace
assumptions here with that real data.

**Claims check.** Suggestions below use only what the site already states:
premium cake boutique, bespoke / designer / wedding cakes, Neha Chaudhary, Greater Noida, Delhi
NCR, Lucknow roots since 2021, WhatsApp ordering, consultations. Before using
words like *delivery*, *eggless*, *same-day*, *price* or specific localities,
**Neha must confirm they are true.**

## 3. New landing pages to build (Phase 4, needs the page builder)

Build a page only when it can be **genuinely useful**, with real photos and real
answers. Thin copies of the same page for every suburb hurt more than they help.

| Priority | Page idea | Why | Needs from Neha |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Wedding cake guide**: "How to plan your wedding cake: tiers, servings and timeline" | Answers questions couples search before choosing a baker; links to `/weddings` and `/consultation` | Her real guidance on servings per tier, lead time and tasting |
| 2 | **Servings guide**: "How big a cake do I need?" | Very common question; helps every category | Her servings/weight chart |
| 3 | **Area page: Greater Noida West** (only if served) | Many societies; people search by locality | Confirm service, and photos of cakes made for that area |
| 4 | **Area page: Noida** (only if served) | Large search area next door | Same as above |
| 5 | **Seasonal**: Diwali hampers / Valentine's bento (only if offered that season) | Time-bound demand; pairs with a short link and GBP post | Confirm the offer and dates |
| 6 | **FAQ page** (with FAQ schema, only for visible answers) | Wins "people also ask" boxes and AI answers | Real answers: lead time, advance, cancellation, eggless (only if true) |

Every new page gets: one clear H1, real photos with alt text, a consultation
and WhatsApp call to action, links to two related pages, and its own SEO title,
description and share image.

## 4. Captions and alt text: use the same words

Search engines, Google Images and AI assistants read words, not pictures. For
each cake post or photo, describe:

**occasion + design + key detail + place**. For example "Three-tier ivory
wedding cake with blush sugar roses for a Greater Noida wedding".

Use that sentence as the Instagram caption opener, the Media alt text, and the
Google Business photo description.

## 5. Signals that matter most for a local bakery (in order)
1. Google Business Profile: complete, active, real reviews (see the playbook).
2. Consistent name, address and phone everywhere.
3. Pages that clearly match the search (wedding page for wedding searches).
4. Real photos, fresh content, fast mobile pages.
5. Mentions and links from local sources: wedding planners, venues,
   photographers and local blogs that genuinely feature Cakeasy work (ask
   partners to credit `@cakeasy99` and link the website).
